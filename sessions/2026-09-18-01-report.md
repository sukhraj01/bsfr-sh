## Findings

- **The template was not a hunt.** `tectonic` (already installed) fetches `IEEEtran.cls` from
  CTAN transparently on first use; total tooling-decision time was under 15 minutes as the brief
  asked, and it produced the *actual* IEEE two-column format rather than a Markdown fallback.
- **Two accidental process-reference leaks, both caught by rereading against the brief's own "what
  must not be in the report" list rather than by any tool.** Worth remembering for any future
  report-writing session in this repo: grep the draft for `CLAUDE.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md`,
  `DEVIATIONS.md`, `sessions/`, and `.py`/`scripts/`-shaped strings before calling a report done,
  since these slip in naturally while drafting from `docs/` (which *does* reference them
  constantly, correctly, for its own purposes).
- **Every number the brief asked for was already sitting in `RESULTS.md` or a `results/tables/*.csv`
  file** — no evidence gap was found that would have required opening a new `PROJECT_STATE.md`
  question about a missing measurement. The one place a number had to be *derived* rather than
  directly copied is the honeypot confusion matrix (TP/FP/FN/TN backed out algebraically from the
  reported precision, recall, and eval-set composition, since no confusion-matrix figure exists in
  `results/`) — labelled explicitly as computed/derived in the table caption, not measured.
- **Page count landed at 9, one page under the 10-12 target**, before any padding was added; three
  rounds of targeted (non-padding) expansion — an engineering-scope paragraph in \S4's intro, a
  paragraph on the paper's own undeclared ML protocol in \S3, and a closing synthesis paragraph in
  \S8 — moved it from 8 to 9 pages and ~5.2K to ~5.8K words. Left here rather than pushed further:
  every remaining candidate addition read as padding rather than argument. Flagged as Q11 in
  `PROJECT_STATE.md` for the next session's judgement call, not resolved unilaterally.
- **Visual proof-reading of the rendered PDF was not completed.** `poppler` (for `pdftoppm`, which
  the `Read` tool needs to rasterize PDF pages) was not preinstalled; a `brew install poppler`
  kicked off mid-session did not finish before the session's token budget favored closing out
  cleanly over waiting on a slow download (the same class of slow-transfer environment issue
  DEV-31 hit on Ada). The LaTeX compile itself is clean (zero errors), and the full `.tex` source
  was read back and reviewed for structural correctness, but a pixel-level "does it look right on
  the page" check is still open — see `PROJECT_STATE.md`'s Next task #1.
